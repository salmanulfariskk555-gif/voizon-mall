01 / RESTAURANT BRANDING & DIGITAL PRESENCEFOOD & HOSPITALITY

Bringing restaurant identity, menus and customer communication together.

Kumbalam, Ernakulam, Kerala

Work discussed for Al-Saj covered a connected set of restaurant marketing and customer-facing materials, from promotional creatives and menu communication to Google Business Profile readiness and group-booking outreach.

Project scope

  • Restaurant branding and promotional poster direction, including breakfast, lunch and dinner content
  • Two-sided table placemat concept with restaurant information, review/menu QR placeholders and kids activity area
  • Menu content preparation for food-ordering platforms such as Petpooja, Swiggy and Zomato
