WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT

What to know before starting a website project

What types of websites can VOIZON discuss?

VOIZON can discuss corporate and business websites, service websites, landing pages, WordPress websites, e-commerce stores and custom web applications. The platform and approach should match the required features, editing needs, budget and long-term maintenance plan.

How much does a business website cost?

Website cost depends on page count, design requirements, content, integrations, e-commerce features, custom functionality, hosting and support. Share a project brief to receive a scope-based quotation rather than relying on a generic price.

How long does website development take?

Timelines depend on project complexity, content readiness, design approvals, integrations and feedback cycles. A delivery schedule and client responsibilities should be agreed in writing before development begins.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive layout checks should be included in the agreed development scope. The website should be reviewed across common mobile, tablet and desktop screen sizes, including navigation, forms, images and key user journeys.

Will my website be SEO-ready?

Basic technical SEO foundations can include descriptive page titles, meta descriptions, crawlable navigation, clean URLs, heading structure, image alt text and sitemap setup. Ongoing SEO strategy and content work are separate unless included in the proposal.

Who owns the website, domain and hosting?

Ownership, administrator access, domain registration, hosting accounts, licences, source files and handover arrangements must be clearly stated in the project agreement. Clients should retain appropriate ownership and access to their business-critical accounts.
