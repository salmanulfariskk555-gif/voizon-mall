COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee leads?

No. Advertising results depend on the offer, market, competition, budget, landing page and customer behaviour. We can plan and optimise campaigns, but cannot guarantee a fixed number of leads.

Is Google Ads spend included in the service fee?

Media spend is normally separate from the service fee and should be agreed clearly in the quotation.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What does Google Ads management include?

Depending on the agreed scope, work may include account and campaign review, keyword and search-intent research, campaign structure, ad copy, location and language settings, conversion tracking review and performance reporting.

Can Google Ads target customers in other countries?

Campaigns can be planned for eligible countries and regions after reviewing the product or service, target audience, language, local advertising requirements, platform policies and budget.

Is the Google Ads budget included in the agency fee?

Advertising spend paid to Google is normally separate from the agency's management fee. The proposal should state the media budget, service fee, payment responsibility and any third-party costs clearly.

Does VOIZON guarantee sales or a specific cost per lead?

No. Campaign results depend on competition, search demand, offer, landing page, conversion tracking, budget and follow-up. We can define measurement and optimisation activities, but cannot guarantee a fixed sales or lead outcome.

What is needed before a Google Ads campaign starts?

Usually the business needs to provide its website or landing page, service details, target locations, budget range, access permissions and conversion goals. Relevant policy, billing and verification requirements must also be satisfied.
