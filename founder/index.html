PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

Business understanding.
Digital execution.

Salmanul Faris K.K. is the Founder of VOIZON Digital Solutions, an agency focused on bringing digital, creative and technology services together for businesses. With an MBA in Data Analytics and a professional focus on digital marketing, he is interested in how strategy, communication, design and technology can work as one coordinated business function.

His approach begins with understanding the organisation: its goals, audience, market position and practical challenges. From there, he works to shape a clear digital direction—whether the requirement involves building a business website, strengthening online visibility, planning a marketing campaign, developing brand communication or coordinating a broader digital project.

At VOIZON, Salmanul’s role centres on business direction, understanding client requirements, shaping project scope and connecting the right mix of capabilities to each engagement. He values clear communication, practical planning, purposeful creative work and delivery aligned with agreed objectives.
