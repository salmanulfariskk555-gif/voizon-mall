COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee leads?

No. Advertising results depend on the offer, market, competition, budget, landing page and customer behaviour. We can plan and optimise campaigns, but cannot guarantee a fixed number of leads.

Is Google Ads spend included in the service fee?

Media spend is normally separate from the service fee and should be agreed clearly in the quotation.
