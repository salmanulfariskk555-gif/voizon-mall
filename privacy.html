LEGAL / YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy.

This page explains how information may be handled when you visit the VOIZON website or contact us about a project.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy applies to the website and project enquiries of VOIZON Digital Solutions, a proprietorship business operating under the trade name VOIZON Digital Solutions. It should be read together with the Terms & Conditions. Project-specific agreements may contain additional data-handling terms.

2. Information you choose to share

If you contact us, you may choose to share your name, business name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country or market, service interest, approximate budget and project details. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through a general enquiry.

3. Website enquiry form

The enquiry form on this website prepares a message and opens WhatsApp for you to review and send. The form does not itself submit the information to a VOIZON website server or store the form entry in a website database. The information is shared with VOIZON only if you choose to send the prepared message or contact us through another channel.

4. WhatsApp and other third-party services

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices. The website may link to third-party services or load third-party resources, such as web fonts. Those providers may process technical information under their own policies. VOIZON does not control their independent data practices.

5. How enquiry information may be used

Information you send to us may be used to respond to your enquiry, understand project requirements, prepare a proposal or quotation, communicate about an agreed project, provide support, maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not promise to use enquiry details for any purpose unrelated to the enquiry or an agreed business relationship.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not offer enquiry information for sale. Information may be shared with relevant project personnel or service providers where reasonably needed to respond to you or deliver an agreed service, or where disclosure is required by law. Any client project material is handled subject to the agreed confidentiality and project arrangements.

7. Retention and security

Information may remain in the communication channel you used and in business records for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, project administration, dispute handling or legal compliance. Appropriate care should be taken when handling such information, but no online communication or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices

You may choose what information to include in an enquiry. You may contact us to request correction or deletion of information held in our own business records, subject to legal, contractual and legitimate record-keeping requirements. Information held by WhatsApp or another third party may need to be managed directly with that provider.

9. Children

This website and our business enquiries are intended for businesses and adults. Please do not submit personal information about a child through the general enquiry form.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or business practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

11. Contact

For a privacy-related question, contact VOIZON Digital Solutions through its official WhatsApp number: +91 95674 97943.

VOIZON Digital Solutions is the trade name supplied for this proprietorship. The proprietor's full legal name and business address have not been supplied and are therefore not published here. This notice describes the current website enquiry flow and should be reviewed against actual business practices and applicable privacy obligations.
