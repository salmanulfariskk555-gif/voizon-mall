SEO / SEARCH VISIBILITY

Questions businesses ask before investing in SEO

What does an SEO agency do for a business website?

An SEO agency reviews how a website can be discovered and understood in organic search. Work may include technical checks, search-intent research, page improvements, content planning, internal linking and performance reporting. The exact plan should be based on the website and business goals.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is an ongoing process, not an instant advertising channel. The time needed to see meaningful changes depends on the website's starting condition, competition, content quality, technical issues and search demand. No fixed ranking timeline should be promised.

Can VOIZON provide local SEO for a business in Kerala?

VOIZON can discuss local SEO tasks such as location-focused service content, Google Business Profile information review, local landing pages and consistency of business details. Access, eligibility and the agreed scope determine which tasks can be completed.

Does SEO include backlinks and blog writing?

Not automatically. Content creation, digital PR, link outreach and other off-page activities should be explicitly listed in the proposal. Any link-building approach should be relevant, transparent and consistent with search engine policies.

What should I share before requesting an SEO proposal?

Share your website URL, main services or products, target locations, priority audience, key competitors if known, and any available Search Console or Analytics access. Never send passwords through a public form.

Does VOIZON guarantee the number-one position on Google?

No. Search rankings are determined by search engine systems and competition. VOIZON can propose measurable SEO work and reporting, but cannot guarantee a specific position, traffic volume or business result.
