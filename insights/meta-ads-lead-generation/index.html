Before You Run Meta Ads: A Lead Generation Checklist for Businesses

Prepare your offer, audience, creative, landing page, follow-up and measurement before spending on Facebook and Instagram ads.

VOIZON Digital Solutions · 7+ Years of Agency Experience

Facebook and Instagram advertising can introduce an offer to relevant audiences and create enquiry opportunities. But campaign setup alone does not guarantee leads or sales. The offer, creative, audience, destination and follow-up process all influence campaign performance.

1. Make the offer clear

Explain what is being offered, who it is for, the main benefit, important conditions and the next step. If the offer is difficult to understand, a larger ad budget will not fix the underlying message.

2. Define the audience and market

Identify the country or service area, customer type, language, age requirements where relevant and the problem the audience is trying to solve. Check platform rules and any local restrictions that apply to the product or service.

3. Choose the right enquiry destination

A campaign may send people to a website landing page, instant form, Messenger, Instagram Direct or WhatsApp where eligible. Choose the destination that the business can respond to reliably and that suits the complexity of the enquiry.

4. Prepare creative variations

Use clear visuals, a direct headline and a specific call to action. Prepare variations to test different messages or formats. Ensure images, claims, prices and testimonials are accurate and approved for use.

5. Check the landing page or messaging journey

Make sure the destination explains the offer, business identity, key details and next step. Test mobile usability, page loading, form fields, WhatsApp links and confirmation messages before launch.

6. Set a realistic test budget

Budget depends on the market, audience, offer and campaign objective. Agree the test duration, spending limit and decision criteria before launch. Advertising spend paid to Meta should be distinguished from agency management fees.

7. Set up measurement and lead handling

Where appropriate, configure events and conversion tracking with the required consent and privacy notices. Decide who responds to leads, how quickly, what questions are asked and how outcomes are recorded. A campaign can generate enquiries that never become sales if follow-up is weak.

8. Review quality as well as cost

Do not judge a campaign only by cost per lead. Review whether enquiries match the target market, can afford the offer, are contactable and progress towards a sale. Use these observations to improve the offer, audience, creative and follow-up.

Common Meta Ads mistakes

Common issues include broad or unclear offers, missing destination testing, weak creative, slow lead response, poor tracking, frequent unplanned edits and treating every enquiry as a qualified prospect. A documented testing plan helps avoid reactive decisions.
