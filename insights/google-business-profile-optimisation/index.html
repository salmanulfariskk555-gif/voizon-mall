Google Business Profile Optimisation: A Business Owner's Checklist

A practical checklist for accurate Google Business Profile categories, services, photos, reviews, hours and performance.

VOIZON Digital Solutions · Updated 2026-11-17

A Google Business Profile can help eligible local businesses appear in Google Search and Maps. A complete profile is useful only when it accurately represents the business and supports a good customer experience. This checklist focuses on information customers rely on.

Represent the business accurately

Use the real business name, the most appropriate category, an accurate address or service-area setup and a phone number the business controls. Follow Google's current eligibility and representation guidelines. Do not create profiles for virtual offices or locations that do not meet eligibility rules.

Choose categories and services carefully

Select the primary category that best describes the main business activity. Add additional categories only when they genuinely apply. List services with clear descriptions and keep availability current.

Keep contact details and hours updated

Check website URL, phone, opening hours, special hours and service information. Make sure the destination website works on mobile and provides consistent details. Incorrect hours can lead to lost enquiries and poor customer experiences.

Use genuine, current photos

Add authentic images of the premises, team, products or work where appropriate and where you have permission. Avoid misleading stock photos that suggest a location, team or facility that does not exist.

Ask for honest reviews

Invite customers to leave honest feedback without incentives or selective review gating. Respond calmly and specifically. Do not disclose private customer information when replying publicly.

Review performance and improve the experience

Use available profile insights alongside website analytics and actual enquiry records. Check which calls, direction requests or website visits lead to meaningful outcomes. For a broader search plan, see VOIZON SEO support.
