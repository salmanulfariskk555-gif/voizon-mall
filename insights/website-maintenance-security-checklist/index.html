Website Maintenance Checklist: Keep Your Business Website Reliable

A practical website maintenance routine covering backups, updates, security, forms, links, performance and content reviews.

VOIZON Digital Solutions · Updated 2026-11-17

Launching a website is the beginning of its operating life, not the end. Regular maintenance helps keep the site secure, accurate and useful as browsers, plugins, content and business requirements change.

Maintain a reliable backup routine

Back up website files and databases at a frequency that reflects how often the site changes. Keep copies separate from the production environment and test restoration periodically. A backup that cannot be restored is not a dependable recovery plan.

Update software carefully

Review CMS core, themes, plugins, dependencies and server software. Apply security updates promptly, remove unused components and check compatibility. Use staging or a rollback plan for higher-risk changes.

Review access and security settings

Use MFA, unique accounts and least-privilege permissions. Remove former staff and vendor access when no longer required. Monitor suspicious login activity and keep domain and hosting accounts protected.

Test important customer journeys

Submit forms, click phone and WhatsApp links, test checkout or booking flows and verify confirmation messages. Check that enquiries reach the right people and that contact details remain accurate.

Check performance and broken links

Review key pages on mobile and desktop, inspect image loading, identify broken internal links and monitor server errors. Use performance measurements to prioritise improvements rather than changing everything at once.

Keep content and policies current

Update service descriptions, team details, opening hours, pricing information where shown and legal policies when operations change. Remove outdated promotions and verify that images and testimonials remain approved.

Document responsibility

Assign an owner for updates, backups, content approvals and incident response. Record what changed and when. Explore website maintenance support if you need an ongoing plan.
