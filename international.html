BEFORE A PROJECT STARTS

Commercial clarity
from the beginning.

International projects may involve additional requirements. These are reviewed and confirmed individually rather than assumed.

Currency & quotation

The quotation currency, pricing basis, validity period and payment milestones are confirmed in writing before acceptance.

Taxes & transfer fees

Applicable taxes, bank charges, payment gateway fees and currency conversion costs are discussed and allocated in the agreement.

Third-party platforms

Advertising budgets, hosting, software subscriptions, stock assets and other external charges are identified separately.

Country requirements

Advertising rules, privacy obligations, data handling, language and local market requirements are considered within the agreed scope. Specialist legal advice may be needed.

INTERNATIONAL PROJECT FAQs

Common questions

Can VOIZON work with clients outside India?

VOIZON welcomes enquiries from businesses in other markets. Project suitability, delivery arrangements and commercial terms are discussed before accepting an engagement.

What information should I send first?

Share your name, company, country, website or social profile (if available), required service, project goal, expected timeline and preferred contact method. A short brief is enough to begin.

How will I know what is included in the price?

The proposal should set out the agreed deliverables, scope boundaries, timeline, quotation currency, payment milestones and any third-party costs before you approve the project.

Who owns my website, accounts and final files?

Ownership, access and handover arrangements should be written into the project agreement. Client-owned domains, platforms and advertising accounts should remain under the client’s control wherever practical.

Can meetings happen online?

Remote meetings and project communication can be arranged by mutual agreement, subject to schedule and time-zone availability.

Can you create campaigns for a specific country?

Country-specific campaigns can be planned after reviewing the target audience, language, offer, platform availability, advertising policies and market requirements.

Which currencies do you accept?

Currency and payment arrangements are confirmed individually in the quotation. Do not assume a payment method or currency until VOIZON confirms it.
