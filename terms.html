LEGAL / PROJECT TERMS

Terms & Conditions.

These terms set out the general basis for project enquiries and services provided under the trade name VOIZON Digital Solutions, a proprietorship business. The signed or accepted quotation, proposal or service agreement for a project will set out its specific scope and commercial details.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Acceptance and project agreement

By accepting a VOIZON quotation, proposal or written project confirmation, the client agrees to the scope, deliverables, fees, payment dates, estimated timeline and any project-specific conditions stated in that document. If a project-specific agreement conflicts with these general terms, the project-specific written agreement will apply to that project.

2. Scope and additional work

Only the deliverables expressly listed in the accepted quotation or agreement are included. New requests, extra revisions, additional pages, integrations, urgent work or changes to an approved brief may require a revised quotation, additional fee or timeline adjustment. Work on additional items begins after written approval.

3. Payment structure

  • 50% advance: The client must pay 50% of the agreed project fee in advance before project work is scheduled or started.
  • Remaining 50%: The balance is payable on the milestone or due date stated in the accepted quotation. Unless a different milestone is agreed in writing, the balance must be cleared before final files, credentials, source files, handover or website launch/publication are released.
  • Separate costs: Advertising spend, hosting, domains, paid software, subscriptions, stock assets, payment gateway charges, taxes and other third-party expenses are separate unless the quotation expressly includes them.
  • Ongoing services: Monthly retainers or recurring services are payable in advance for each billing period if the quotation says so.

All amounts, currency, applicable taxes, payment method and invoice due dates must be stated in the quotation or invoice. A payment is treated as received when cleared funds are credited to the designated account or payment channel.

4. Work commencement and client cooperation

Work begins after the agreed advance is received and the client has supplied the essential content, access, approvals and instructions needed to proceed. The client is responsible for ensuring that supplied text, images, logos, data and other materials may lawfully be used. Delays in feedback, approvals, access or content may move the delivery schedule and any dependent milestones.

5. Due dates and late payments

The client must pay each invoice by its stated due date. If payment is overdue, VOIZON may send a reminder and, after written notice, pause work, campaign management, support or delivery until the overdue amount is cleared. Project timelines and reserved resources may be rescheduled to reflect the delay.

No late fee or penalty is charged under VOIZON's standard payment policy. If an invoice remains unpaid after its due date, VOIZON may send a payment reminder and pause project work, campaign management, support or delivery after notifying the client. Work will resume after the overdue amount is received, subject to resource availability and a revised schedule where necessary. Any different arrangement must be expressly agreed in writing before the project begins.

Where the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006 applies to the supplier and transaction, its mandatory delayed-payment provisions may apply notwithstanding a different contractual term. Eligible micro and small enterprise suppliers should obtain advice on registration status and statutory requirements before relying on those provisions.

6. Cancellation, advance and refunds

The client may request cancellation in writing. Once work has started, the advance is applied against work completed, time reasonably spent, approved commitments and non-cancellable third-party costs incurred for the project. Fees for work already completed, delivered or performed and third-party costs already committed are not refundable.

VOIZON does not provide an automatic or unconditional refund merely because a client changes their mind, delays approvals, does not use a completed deliverable or cancels after work has been performed. If an amount remains unearned after accounting for work performed and recoverable commitments, its treatment will be determined under the accepted project agreement and applicable law. Nothing in this clause removes any refund or other right that cannot legally be excluded.

Any cancellation charge or amount retained must be reasonable, disclosed in the project agreement and proportionate to the work performed and costs incurred. The specific cancellation and refund position for a project should be recorded in its quotation before payment.

7. Revisions, approvals and delivery

The number of included revision rounds, approval deadlines, delivery format and handover requirements will be stated in the quotation or project brief. The client must review drafts and provide consolidated feedback within the agreed period. Approval of a design, content, campaign or website milestone confirms acceptance of that milestone, subject to any defects or rights that cannot legally be waived.

8. Ownership and portfolio confidentiality

Unless the project agreement says otherwise, final bespoke deliverables are transferred or licensed to the client only after all amounts due for the relevant project have been paid in full. VOIZON retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates, methods and know-how. Third-party assets remain subject to their own licence terms.

VOIZON will not publicly display client names, confidential project information or project samples without the client's permission or other lawful authority. A client may separately authorise specific work for portfolio use in writing.

9. Third-party platforms and results

Services may depend on platforms, hosting providers, advertising networks, domain registrars, software vendors or other third parties. Their availability, policies, pricing and decisions are outside VOIZON's direct control. Unless expressly guaranteed in a signed agreement, VOIZON does not guarantee specific search rankings, advertising results, sales, leads, traffic or revenue.

10. Client responsibilities and lawful use

The client must provide accurate information, lawful content, required permissions and timely decisions. The client is responsible for the accuracy of business claims, prices, offers, regulated-industry statements and legal notices supplied for publication. The client must not request unlawful, infringing, deceptive or harmful work.

11. Confidentiality

Each party should protect non-public business, customer, technical and commercial information received from the other party and use it only for the project, except where disclosure is authorised or legally required. Any additional confidentiality or data-processing obligations should be recorded in a separate written agreement where needed.

12. Limitation and events outside control

To the extent permitted by law, neither party is responsible for delay caused by events reasonably beyond its control, including major service outages, network failures, natural events, government action or third-party platform disruption. The affected party should communicate the issue and reasonable next steps. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

13. Changes and disputes

Changes to a project scope, price, milestone or payment date should be agreed in writing. If a concern arises, both parties should first try to resolve it through good-faith discussion. Applicable law and the appropriate forum will be determined by the parties' project agreement and mandatory law; this general page does not attempt to select a court where VOIZON's legal business details have not yet been confirmed.
