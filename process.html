OUR WORKING PROCESS

Clear steps.
Collaborative delivery.

Every project is different. Our process provides a clear framework so requirements, scope, responsibilities and next steps can be discussed before work begins.

01

Discover

We learn about your business, audience, current digital presence, goals and challenges.

02

Define

We agree on the priority, recommended approach, deliverables, timeline and information needed.

03

Plan

We organise the work into practical tasks, content requirements, approvals and milestones.

04

Create

We develop the agreed designs, content, website components or campaign setup.

05

Review

You review the work and provide feedback against the agreed scope.

06

Launch

Approved work is prepared for publishing, handover or campaign activation.

07

Measure

Where tracking is available, we review performance data against agreed objectives.

08

Improve

We identify useful next steps, refinements and future opportunities.

PROJECT CLARITY

What we align
before starting.

Before a project begins, we aim to confirm the required service, deliverables, timeline, pricing, payment terms, content and asset responsibilities, approval process and communication channel in writing. The exact scope is agreed in the project proposal or quotation.
