OUR WORKING PROCESS

Clear steps.
Collaborative delivery.

Every project is different. Our process provides a clear framework so requirements, scope, responsibilities and next steps can be discussed before work begins.

01

Discover

We learn about your business, audience, current digital presence, goals and challenges.

02

Define

We agree on the priority, recommended approach, deliverables, timeline and information needed.

03

Plan

We organise the work into practical tasks, content requirements, approvals and milestones.

04

Create

We develop the agreed designs, content, website components or campaign setup.

05

Review

You review the work and provide feedback against the agreed scope.

06

Launch

Approved work is prepared for publishing, handover or campaign activation.

07

Measure

Where tracking is available, we review performance data against agreed objectives.

08

Improve

We identify useful next steps, refinements and future opportunities.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A clear proposal.
A shared plan.

Before you approve a project, the proposal or quotation should make the important details easy to understand.

Project understanding

A summary of your goals, audience, requirements and the problem the project is intended to address.

Scope & deliverables

A practical list of included pages, designs, campaigns, outputs, integrations or services, plus exclusions.

Timeline & milestones

Estimated stages, review points, client inputs and expected approval dates.

Commercial details

Fees, quotation currency, payment milestones, validity period and separate third-party costs.

PROJECT ONBOARDING

Ready to get started?

Once the proposal is approved, both sides align on the essentials before production begins.

01

Confirm the agreement

Approve the written proposal, scope, payment terms and project-specific conditions.

02

Complete the advance

Pay the agreed advance and confirm the cleared payment before work is scheduled.

03

Share project inputs

Provide brand assets, content, access and decisions through an agreed secure channel.

04

Confirm kickoff

Agree the point of contact, communication channel, meeting window and first milestone.

DELIVERY & HANDOVER

Know what happens
at every stage.

Progress updates

Updates are shared through the agreed communication channel at the agreed review points.

Feedback & revisions

Review drafts against the approved scope and provide consolidated feedback within the agreed review window.

Final review

Check approved deliverables, functionality and agreed acceptance items before closure.

Handover

Final files, credentials, source files or launch support are handled according to the agreement and payment completion.

PROJECT CLARITY

What we align
before starting.

Before a project begins, we aim to confirm the required service, deliverables, timeline, pricing, payment terms, content and asset responsibilities, approval process and communication channel in writing. The exact scope is agreed in the project proposal or quotation.
